Goal

Replace the practice's five-year-old site with a calm, bookable-first site. Patients should find opening hours and book a check-up in under a minute on a phone.

Scope

Home, treatments (nine pages), team, prices, contact and a booking flow connected to the existing calendar. Copy is supplied by the client; photography is a separate task.

Deliverables

Sitemap and wireframes, two visual routes, a clickable prototype, the built site and a short editor handbook.

Timeline

Kick-off 12 Oct, first route 28 Oct, prototype 11 Nov, launch 12 Dec. Invoices follow the three milestones.
