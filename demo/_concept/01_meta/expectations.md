# Expectations

## The result

- **Judged by**: Maria stops being asked "where do we eat?": the team opens a poll instead of a chat thread. (user, 2026-10-23)
- **Quality bar**: a Member votes from the phone in one tap, and every Member sees the same result. (user, 2026-10-23)
- **Liked**: Wheel of Names — the draw happens in front of everyone. (user, 2026-10-23)
- **Disliked**: Doodle — a lunch place is not a slot in a date grid. (user, 2026-10-23)
- **Must not happen**: the organizer's choice wins in disguise, through a hidden count or a hidden draw. (user, 2026-10-23)
