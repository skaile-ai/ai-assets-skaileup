# Goals

**The one outcome.** A team gets from "where do we eat?" to a settled pick without a chat thread, and trusts the result because every vote and every draw is visible.

**Signals.** Most polls close with three or more votes, and the first vote lands within ten minutes of opening a poll.

**Constraints.** A web app that works on a phone first, for teams of up to fifteen. It picks a place and stops; it does not order or book.

**Non-goals.** Ordering, booking, rating places after the visit and dietary filtering beyond a member's own diet label. People will expect these; this deliberately does not do them.
