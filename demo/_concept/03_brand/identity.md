# Identity

## Direction
A warm, quick and slightly cheeky lunch companion. The product decides small things, so it must feel light: big round shapes, a single bright accent and no corporate grey. It fits a team tool people open for ten seconds before eating.

## Colour
- **Tomato** (`color.primary`, #e4572e): the one primary action on a screen and the draw.
- **Basil** (`color.secondary`, #2f7d4f): votes that are cast, success states.
- **Saffron** (`color.accent`, #f2b134): the memorable element, only on the pick.
- **Cream** (`color.background`, #fff8ee) and **Linen** (`color.surface`, #ffffff): page and raised surface.
- **Espresso** (`color.text`, #2b2118) and **Mocha** (`color.text-muted`, #6f6256) for text; **Oat** (`color.border`, #e8dccb) for dividers.
- Error #c0392b, success #2f7d4f, warning #d68910.

## Type
Headings in Fraunces, body in Inter, monospace in JetBrains Mono for counts. Page title 28 px, section title 20 px, body 16 px, label 13 px.

## Spacing and elevation
An 8 px unit. Cards sit on three soft elevations: sm for rows, md for the poll card, lg for the draw overlay.

## Atmosphere
Flat colour with a faint paper grain on the cream background; no gradients.

## Memorable element
The **draw**: the tied places spin as a small roulette of saffron chips and the pick lands with a short bounce, so a tie feels like an event the whole team watches rather than a rule the app applied.

## Tone and copy
Friendly, brief, a little playful. Friendly: first names, no "user". Brief: a button says what it does ("Open poll", "Draw the pick"). Playful: an empty list says "No polls yet - hungry?". It rules out: jargon, exclamation marks in errors, and the words "submit", "entity" and "participant".
