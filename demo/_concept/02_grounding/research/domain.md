# Domain: team lunch decisions

## Terminology
- **Poll**: a time-boxed question with a fixed set of options and one vote per participant. Source: [Doodle help, "How polls work"](https://help.doodle.com/).
- **Tiebreak**: a rule applied when options end with equal votes. Collective-decision tools settle it with a visible random draw or a second round. Source: [Robert's Rules of Order, "Tie vote"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert%27s_Rules_of_Order).

## Regulations
- Lunch choices touch dietary needs, not health data: a stated diet ("vegetarian") is a preference the app stores, and in the EU a stated diet can reveal religion or health, so the member sets it themselves and may leave it at "Any". Source: [GDPR Art. 9](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-9-gdpr/).

## Trends
- Teams vote inside the chat tool they already use, and polls there go stale because nobody closes them. Source: [Slack Help, "Polls and surveys"](https://slack.com/help).

## Existing workflows
- A message in the team chat asks "where?", three people answer, and the first confident voice wins. The alternative is a shared map list that records candidates and never decides. Source: [Google Maps Help, "Save places in lists"](https://support.google.com/maps).
