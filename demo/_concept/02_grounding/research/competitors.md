# Competitors

## Doodle
- Features: one-link polls, no account needed to vote, calendar sync. Source: [doodle.com](https://doodle.com).
- Strengths: the link is the whole onboarding.
- Gaps users complain about: a date grid is the only model; a place with a cuisine or a walking time is not supported. Not publicly available: a tiebreak rule.
- Pricing: free tier with ads, paid tiers per organizer. Source: [doodle.com/pricing](https://doodle.com/pricing).
- Audience: anyone scheduling a meeting.
- For this product: borrow the link-first vote, avoid the date grid.

## Slack polls
- Features: an emoji-reaction or app poll posted in a channel. Source: [Slack Help](https://slack.com/help).
- Strengths: the vote happens where the team already talks.
- Gaps: no deadline that closes the poll; no pick. Not publicly available: a stored list of places.
- Pricing: included in paid Slack plans, apps vary.
- Audience: teams on Slack.
- For this product: borrow the in-context vote, add the deadline and the draw.

## Wheel of Names
- Features: a random wheel over typed names, shareable result. Source: [wheelofnames.com](https://wheelofnames.com).
- Strengths: the draw is visible, so the result feels fair.
- Gaps: no vote behind the wheel, so every option weighs the same.
- Pricing: free.
- Audience: classrooms, giveaways, small teams.
- For this product: borrow the visible draw, use it only to break a tie.
