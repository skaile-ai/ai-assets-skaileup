# Audiences

## Maria Schmidt, Office Manager
- Tools today: the team's chat tool, a shared Google Maps list, a spreadsheet of birthdays.
- Pain points: she is asked "where do we eat?" every day and ends up deciding; she gets blamed when the place is wrong.
- Design implications: opening a poll must take under a minute; the result must read as the team's choice, not hers. Source: onboarding answers, `audience` question, 2026-10-24.

## Jean-Luc Moreau, Backend Developer
- Tools today: the team's chat tool on his phone, a vegetarian diet.
- Pain points: he votes late because the thread is buried; he is offered a steakhouse as the only option.
- Design implications: a vote is one tap on a phone; a member's diet filters what the poll offers. Source: onboarding answers, `audience` question, 2026-10-24.
