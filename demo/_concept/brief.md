# Lunch Roulette

Lunch Roulette is a lunch planner for small teams. Someone opens a **poll** for a day, every **member** votes for the **places** they would go to, and when the deadline passes the app announces the **pick**. When two places tie, the app **draws** one at random, in public, so nobody has to decide and nobody can claim they were overruled.

A team keeps its own list of places with the cuisine and the walk from the office door, so a poll offers real options in seconds and a vegetarian never gets a steakhouse as the only choice.

## Comparables

- **Doodle**: borrow the one-link poll that needs no account to read; avoid its generic date grid, which has no notion of a place or a walk.
- **Slack polls**: borrow the vote inside the channel where the team already talks; avoid the pile of emoji reactions that nobody ever closes.
- **Google Maps lists**: borrow the shared list of places with distance; avoid the lack of any decision step, because a list never picks.
- **Wheel of Names**: borrow the visible random draw that makes the result feel fair; avoid the blank wheel with no vote behind it.

The gap: nothing combines a team's own place list, a vote with a deadline and a visible tiebreak in one place.
