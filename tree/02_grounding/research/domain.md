# _concept/02_grounding/research/domain.md

What the domain already knows: its vocabulary, the regulations that constrain the product, the
trends shaping it, and the workflows people in it already follow. A regulation missed here
surfaces later as a rewrite of the data model.

## Format

```yaml
---
last_updated: YYYY-MM-DD
---
```

The body has one section each for terminology, regulations, trends and existing workflows, with
a source against every factual claim.

## Writing it

1. Take the domain from the user's answers on a first run, from `brief.md` on a re-run.
2. Dispatch the topic per `tree/02_grounding/research/README.md` and write the returned
   findings, primary sources first.

## Checks

Every factual claim names its source.
